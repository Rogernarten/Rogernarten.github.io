Point of view

The limit of AI in business decisions is trust, not technology.

At Amazon I spent six months working side by side with sourcing managers. Every week, one of them spent more than ten hours pulling and analyzing data before deciding where to focus. The first time the manager ran the AI tool I had built and got precise guidance for the week in five minutes, the excitement was obvious. Today the tool is standard across the team.

It worked because, before building any AI, I fixed the data foundation, and then designed the agent so that every number comes from verified data rather than from the model. As AI blurs the line between business and engineering, I want to be the person who understands both.

A voice tutor that teaches empathy to autistic children

AI Software Engineer · ~500 users · 600+ voice sessions a day · OpenAI Realtime & Gemini Live
Conversation design
  • Wrote every voice-tutor prompt. The model never grades the child itself: it must call an evaluate() tool and obey the result.
  • A short spoken preamble before each tool call masks latency; one idea per turn keeps responses fast and child-friendly.
  • Answer options are locked and position-randomized so children cannot guess patterns.

Linguistics · Fudan University (2025)

Time as space: “before” and “after” in German and Chinese

B.A. thesis, written in German (53 pages) · cognitive linguistics

Compared how German vor / hinter / nach and Chinese 前 / 后 express both space and time, arguing from philosophy, neuroscience, developmental psychology and etymology that our sense of time grows out of our sense of space.

Findings: both languages extend “before” into time far more richly than “after”, and both mainly use a Moving-Time model, while English mixes models — a mixed pattern now spreading into both languages.

Why it matters for AI

“Move the meeting forward” is ambiguous in English — earlier or later? — precisely because two time models collide. A voice assistant that schedules across languages has to resolve exactly these expressions.
